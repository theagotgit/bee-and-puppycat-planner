# ✦ Temp Job Log

A cute, simple work log that runs in your browser. Log the hours you spend on each job, track your chargeability, and keep a to-do list. There's also a list of things to bring up with people the next time you talk to them.

It's a single file. There's nothing to install, no account to create, and no server.

## Get started

1. Click the green **Code** button at the top of this page, then **Download ZIP**.
2. Find the downloaded file (usually in your *Downloads* folder) and unzip it. On Windows, right-click it and choose **Extract All**. On a Mac, double-click it.
3. Open the unzipped folder and double-click **`worklog.html`**. It opens in your web browser.

That's it! Tip: bookmark the page so it's easy to find again.

## What's in it

**Temp Jobs**
- Add a row for each thing you work on, with a start time, an end time, the customer and what you did.
- Tick **Non-chargeable** for work that can't be billed to a customer, such as internal meetings or admin.
- The dashboard shows your hours for the day and your chargeability (the share of hours that aren't non-chargeable).

**To Do**
- A list with optional deadlines. Tasks due today turn yellow and overdue tasks turn red.

**People**
- Write down things to bring up the next time you talk to someone, like your manager, a colleague or a customer.
- Anyone with open points shows up on the dashboard, so you don't forget.

## Add your own pictures 🖼️

Each page has a picture box in the top-right corner. To add your own picture (a pet, a favourite character, anything you like), do one of these:

- **Click the picture box** and choose an image, or
- **Drag an image** from your computer onto the box, or
- Click **⚙️ Settings** at the top of the page. You can change or remove pictures there.

## Good to know

- **Your data stays on your computer.** Everything is saved in your browser, and nothing is sent anywhere. (The only thing the page downloads from the internet is its fonts.)
- **Use the same browser each time.** Edge, Chrome, Firefox and Safari each keep their own copy, so your log won't follow you between browsers or computers.
- **Clearing your browsing data deletes your log.** If you clear "site data" or "cookies and other site data", the log and pictures are wiped too.
- **Moving `worklog.html` to another folder** can make the browser treat it as a new page with an empty log. Pick a home for it before you start using it.
- **Dark mode:** click **🌙 Space Outside** at the top. Click **☀️ Back to Earth** to switch back.
